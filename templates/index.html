How It Works

From upload to explainable prediction

A simple, four-step pipeline behind every scan you submit.

1. Upload

Upload a JPG/PNG MRI brain scan through the web interface.

2. Preprocess

The image is resized, normalized, and prepared for the model.

3. Classify

A CNN / EfficientNetB0 model predicts the Alzheimer's stage with a confidence score.

4. Explain

Grad-CAM highlights the brain regions that most influenced the prediction.

Classification Output

Four stages. One model.

01
Non-Demented

No significant signs of Alzheimer's-related structural change.

02
Very Mild Demented

Subtle early structural changes consistent with the earliest stage.

03
Mild Demented

Moderate structural changes consistent with the mild stage.

04
Moderate Demented

Pronounced structural changes consistent with the advanced stage.

Why It Matters

Alzheimer's disease affects millions worldwide. Manual MRI interpretation is time-consuming and dependent on specialist availability. This system supports — not replaces — clinicians with fast, consistent, and interpretable predictions.

Explainable, Not a Black Box

Every prediction ships with a Grad-CAM heatmap overlay, so the regions driving the model's decision are visible — improving transparency and clinical trust.
